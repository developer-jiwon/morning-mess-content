Morning Mess Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Won&Tone / Effective: October 28, 2026

This policy is for consumers in Washington State. It describes the consumer health data, as defined in the Washington My Health My Data Act (chapter 19.373 RCW), that the Morning Mess app collects. It is separate from our Privacy Policy.

1. Consumer health data we collect and why

If you allow it, the app reads on your iPhone today's total step count from the Apple Health app (HealthKit), including steps recorded by Apple Watch, and today's step count from Motion & Fitness. While you tidy the house in the app, it also counts jumps, turns and squats from your iPhone's motion sensors. The app uses this data only to check walking and movement quests in the app. Only today's totals are kept, on your device, and they start again each day. The app does not write any data to the Health app.

2. Sources of consumer health data

The Apple Health app and the pedometer and motion sensors of your iPhone.

3. Consumer health data we share

None. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we do not sell it. If you turn on device backup (including iCloud Backup), today's totals may be included in your own device backup with Apple.

4. Third parties and affiliates we share consumer health data with

None. We have no affiliates.

5. How to exercise your rights

You can confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, access it, withdraw consent, and have it deleted. Because the data is only on your device:

  • To stop collection, turn off access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness and in the Health app under your profile > Apps > Morning Mess.
  • To delete it, delete the app. A copy in your device backup can be deleted under iCloud Backup in your iPhone's Settings.

You can also email dev.jiwonnie@gmail.com to make any of these requests. We do not require an account. We will respond within 45 days of receiving your request; if we need more time, we will tell you within those 45 days, with the reason, and respond within a further 45 days. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our response. We will tell you the result of your appeal in writing within 45 days, and if we deny it, we will tell you how to contact the Washington State Attorney General to submit a complaint.
